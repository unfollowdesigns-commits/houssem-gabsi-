The challenge

Access to AI, little practical value.

He had the AI tools. He just wasn't getting much out of them, because the defaults had nothing to do with how his week actually runs.

So every recurring task started from a blank page, and the result was a little different each week.

The approach

Set up around his week, not the software's defaults.

  1. 01

    Map the editorial week.

    The tasks he repeats every week were written down, in the order he does them.

  2. 02

    Write each task once.

    Each recurring task became a set of instructions the assistant follows the same way every time.

  3. 03

    Video at the centre.

    The setup is built around video production, the core of his work.

  4. 04

    The judgement stays his.

    The assistant prepares. Editorial decisions remain his.
