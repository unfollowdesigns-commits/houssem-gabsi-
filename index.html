A recruiting website that keeps itself current

Executive search firm, Washington D.C., since 2025

Challenge

The firm had outgrown its website. Open roles were maintained manually in two places, candidates were assessed one at a time, and client reports were assembled by hand.

Work delivered

An eighteen-page website with open roles synchronised from the firm's recruiting system. A tool that formats candidate CVs to the firm's standard. An interactive map of its consultant network. The firm's recruiting database and internal chat connected to its AI assistant, so the team can query its own candidate data.

Status

Website live. Candidate scoring in development: each candidate assessed against the criteria of the search, with written reasoning for every score.

A recruiting platform that explains every score

Own product, 2026

Visit vero-woad.vercel.app
Challenge

Most candidate screening tools return a score without the reasoning behind it, so a recruiter can neither defend it nor question it.

Work delivered

Vero, a web application that reads a batch of résumés against a role, ranks the candidates, and shows the reasoning and the source text behind every score. It also drafts outreach based on each candidate's own work. The decision stays with a person.

Status

Live.

A daily entry-risk score for stocks and crypto

Own product, 2026

Visit zonedinout.vercel.app
Challenge

A price shows what an asset costs today, not whether today is a sensible moment to buy it.

Work delivered

ZonedInOut, a website that scores stocks, ETFs and cryptocurrencies every trading day on how far the price has moved from its average, with an entry zone and a stop level for each. Past calls are published and graded. It is a scoring system, not financial advice.

Status

Live.
