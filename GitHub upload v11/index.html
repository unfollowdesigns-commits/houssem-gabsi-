02 · About

AI made content cheap.
Taste is what's scarce now.

I'm a producer for established firms. I build the systems a firm runs on (websites, follow-ups, briefs, content) and make the photos and films that show it. Trained at EFA. Media Producer at BBC Media Action, 2019 to 2020.

How I work

  1. AuditA paid look at where the firm loses time and clients, with a ranked plan.
  2. BuildA fixed price agreed before anything starts. 50% upfront.
  3. LaunchYour team is shown how it works. Nothing runs without someone who owns it.
  4. CareOptional monthly support: fixes, improvements, and content through the Content Engine.

Questions

What does a Systems Audit include?

A kick-off call, a look at the tools you choose to show, and a designed report: where the firm loses time and clients, what each leak costs in your own numbers, and the first three fixes ranked. The fee counts toward the build.

Will AI send anything without us checking?

No. Every system I build drafts and prepares. A person on your team approves before anything reaches a client.

Which tools do you work with?

The ones you already use where possible: your email, calendar, CRM and spreadsheets. I build with n8n, Claude, Framer and Vercel.

Do you work with firms outside Tunisia?

Yes. Most of my work is for clients abroad, in English or French. Calls are scheduled in your time zone.

How is the price set?

After the audit, every build gets one fixed price, agreed in writing before work starts.
